**Menus, a title screen, and Settings.** The game now opens on a proper title screen, has a new pause menu, and a full Settings screen that remembers your choices on this device.

### What to try

- **First launch:** a short photosensitivity notice, then the title screen. Tap anywhere (or press Enter) to start.
- **Pause:** press Esc, P, or Start (or the pause button on screen). Resume, Restart level, Settings, and Quit to title (it asks first).
- **Settings:** the gear on the title screen, or Settings in the pause menu. Try:
  - Audio: volume sliders, mono audio, and Night mode (evens out loud and quiet sounds).
  - Graphics: quality preset, a 30 or 60 fps cap, and Show FPS.
  - Controls: stick deadzone and feel, button size and opacity, Hold or Toggle shooting.
  - Gameplay: difficulty, assist options (invulnerability, infinite jetpack fuel, slower game speed, auto-fire), camera shake, screen flashes, damage numbers.
  - Accessibility: text size and reduce motion.
- Close the game and open it again: your settings are still there.
- Some rows say they arrive later (for example captions, colorblind palettes, and the touch layout editor). Your choice is kept until then.

### Install on Android

1. Tap `cosmic-drift-0.0.5.apk` below to download it.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to "install unknown apps".
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**. It installs over the older version.
