**Test arena build.** The first playable version: one test level to check movement and controls. No enemies, sound, or menus yet.

### What to try

- **Touch:** put your left thumb anywhere in the bottom-left area to move (the stick appears where you touch). Use the buttons on the right: JUMP, DASH, FIRE, PARRY, GEAR. Try holding the stick and pressing JUMP and DASH at the same time.
- **Jump:** tap for a small hop, hold for a full jump. Keep holding after the top of the jump to use the jetpack.
- **Wall jump:** in the tall shaft, push into a wall while falling, then press JUMP.
- **Drop through** the thin platforms: hold down on the stick and press JUMP.
- **Controller:** if you have one, connect it by Bluetooth. A jumps, B dashes, the left stick or D-pad moves, Start pauses.
- **Pause:** the round **II** button top-right.

### Install on Android

1. Tap `cosmic-drift-0.0.2.apk` below to download it.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to "install unknown apps".
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**. It installs over 0.0.1.
