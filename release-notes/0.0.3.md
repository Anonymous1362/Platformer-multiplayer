**Art, HUD, and sound.** The test arena now has its space look (nebula sky, city silhouettes, glowing platform edges, a real astronaut and Crawler), an on-screen HUD, a pause menu with Restart, effects, and sound.

### What to try

- **Look around:** the background moves at different depths as you run. Shards spin, the Crawler glows orange just before it charges.
- **HUD (top-left):** the white bar is health, the cyan strip on top is your shield, the gold bar below is jetpack fuel. Top-right counts your shards.
- **Sound:** tap or press any key once to turn sound on (phones require a first touch). Jump, shoot, dash, collect shards (the pitch rises as you chain them), get hit, reach a checkpoint.
- **Fire button:** a ring fills as the blaster heats up and turns red while it vents. The DASH ring shows the cooldown.
- **Pause:** the round **II** button, Esc, or Start. Choose **Resume** or **Restart level**.
- **Low health:** below a quarter of your health the screen edges pulse red and you hear a heartbeat.
- **Reduced motion:** if your phone has "Remove animations" on, the shake is much smaller and blinking is replaced by a steady outline.

### Install on Android

1. Tap `cosmic-drift-0.0.3.apk` below to download it.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to "install unknown apps".
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**. It installs over the older version.
