**New art and real sounds.** The astronaut, the Crawler, the shards, the platform walls, and the city skyline are now 3D-rendered art made in Blender for this game. The astronaut is animated, and the game plays real recorded sound effects.

### What to try

- **Astronaut:** watch the animations for standing (breathing), running, jumping up, falling, dashing, wall sliding, the jetpack, and getting hit. The cyan parts on the suit show your player colour.
- **Crawler:** it walks on six legs now, and still glows orange just before it charges.
- **Shards:** faceted gold crystals that spin.
- **Walls and floors:** metal panels with bolts, vents, and small lights.
- **Sound:** tap once to turn sound on. You now hear footsteps while running, metal clanks when landing, laser shots, explosions when a Crawler is destroyed, and a power-up chime at checkpoints.

### Install on Android

1. Tap `cosmic-drift-0.0.4.apk` below to download it.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to "install unknown apps".
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**. It installs over the older version.
